Dear Sir & Madam,

Teacher's Day ❤️

Happy Teacher's Day to both of you, Sir and Madam! 🌸

Even though we are now far away from each other as teachers and students, I still remember those two years so clearly.

Those two years were some of the best years of my life. They were such beautiful years because, during that time, I learned so many things—not only about my studies, but also about life.

It has only been five months since I left, but sometimes it feels like such a long time.

My friends are now studying in the English Diploma and Certificate courses, and they often tell me about both of you and our other lecturers. Sometimes I tell them, "Okay, stop!" 😄

Because whenever I start remembering those days, I become emotional. I miss those memories, and somehow they also remind me of my home, Mom and Dad, and the beautiful memories connected with those two years.

When I think about how both of you came into my life, I realize how special those memories are. They are some of the happiest and most emotional memories I have.

When I was leaving your place, Madam, you even gave me a little plan for my future. 😄 I still haven't made any decision about marriage. Honestly, I feel marriage is a little scary! 😂 Let's see what happens in the future.

Whenever I tell stories about my life to my cousins, I always end up telling them stories about both of you. And the funny thing is, whenever I start one of those stories, they already know who I am talking about. 😄

These days, I have started creating small projects related to my career. This Teacher's Day card is also one of them.

When I left your place, I had never created projects like this. But I promised myself that maybe, even if we didn't meet for a long time, after I started building my practical skills, I would definitely create a special wishing card for both of you.

I worked hard on this for the last few months, and now I can finally create my own small projects.

At the moment, I am preparing for my final-year project while staying in Gampaha under my brother's guidance. I am also looking for an internship related to my degree.

I am slowly collecting knowledge, experience, and values for my future career.

When I was studying at your place during my diploma, that was a completely different time in my life. I could give almost all of my attention to studying.

But now, things are different.

Now I have to think about finding a job, looking after my family, completing my academic work, working on projects, and preparing myself for my future career.

For me, all of this is new.

And even though life is moving forward, I still miss my family and those two beautiful years.

Sometimes, when I feel tired or feel like giving up, I still remember Madam's voice.

Madam, your voice still gives me discipline. ❤️

That is something I don't think I will ever forget.

I am truly grateful for both of you and for all the memories you gave me. That time was truly a marvelous part of my life.

Thank you for creating such a beautiful period in my memory.

And because of both of you, I became more confident about speaking English. Without fear, I started talking in English, and now I am even trying to do English voice-overs step by step in my vlogs.

The funny thing is, these days I am so busy with academics and projects that I sometimes don't even find enough time to edit my Sinhala vlogs! 😄

But I am trying to keep moving forward, step by step.

❤️ One more thing...

I still want to talk with Madam sometimes, but I always think that maybe I might disturb both of you.

So these are some of the things I wanted to tell you.

Thank you, Sir and Madam, for all the guidance, encouragement, discipline, kindness, and beautiful memories you gave me.

I may have left your place, but I don't think I will ever leave those memories behind.

You are both truly the best teachers I have had in my life.

And even today, I don't think anyone could replace the place both of you have in my heart.

🌷 Happy Teacher's Day once again, Sir and Madam!

Thank you for being such an important part of my life.

May God bless both of you with happiness, good health, and a beautiful life together. ❤️

With love, respect, and heartfelt gratitude,

Lashi 🌸
