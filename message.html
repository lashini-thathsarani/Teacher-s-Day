Happy Teacher's Day to both of you, Sir and Madam! 🌸

When I think about how both of you came into my life, I realize how special those memories are. They are some of the happiest and most emotional memories I have.

Sometimes, when I feel tired or feel like giving up, I still remember Madam's voice.

Madam, your voice still gives me discipline. ❤️

That is something I don't think I will ever forget.

I am truly grateful for both of you and for all the memories you gave me. That time was truly a marvelous part of my life.

Thank you for creating such a beautiful period in my memory.

And because of both of you, I became more confident about speaking English. Without fear, I started talking in English, and now I am even trying to do English voice-overs step by step in my vlogs.

I still want to talk with Madam sometimes, but I always think that maybe I might disturb both of you. So these are some of the things I wanted to tell you.

Thank you, Sir and Madam, for all the guidance, encouragement, discipline, kindness, and beautiful memories you gave me. I may have left your place, but I don't think I will ever leave those memories behind. You are both truly the best teachers I have had in my life. And even today, I don't think anyone could replace the place both of you have in my heart.

🌷 Happy Teacher's Day once again, Sir and Madam!
